The architecture

Private by construction, not by promise.

Privacy is not a settings toggle bolted onto a cloud app. It is the shape of the system: local-first storage, narrow OAuth scope, encrypted archives, and entitlements that keep working when the signal does not.

100% offline-first

Your journal opens in airplane mode. Pages live in a local SQLite + Drift engine with SQLCipher encryption — no server round-trip, no cloud lock-in, no spinner between you and the page.

SQLite / DriftSQLCipherAirplane ready

Private Drive vault

A hidden drive.appdata archive encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The DEK is wrapped by our server’s Master KEK and strictly bound to your authenticated backup_drive_email.

drive.appdataAES-256-GCMEnveloped DEK

Biometric sanctuary gate

Fingerprint or face unlock on cold start, backed by the device’s hardware keystore. The journal key does not leave the phone just because the screen turned off.

FingerprintFace unlockHardware keystore

Ed25519 offline leases

Trial and entitlement leases are signed on the server and verified locally. No nagging internet checks mid-sentence, and existing entries are never locked from reading.
